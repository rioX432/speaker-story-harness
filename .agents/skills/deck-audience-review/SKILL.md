# Blind listener assessment, after production

Adapted from the Kotlin Fest `deck-audience-review` skill. Use a fresh reviewer with no access to the author story plan, future slides, answer rubric, raw evidence ledger or earlier reviewer notes.

Give only the listener profile and sequential slide text/images plus the speaker's actual spoken lines. Reveal material in chronological sections, freezing each response before showing later sections. Ask: what problem is being solved, why was this remedy considered, what changed in the experiment, and what would you choose in a different scenario? Require citations to actually seen slide/notes, and mark unstated guesses as guesses.

A separate grader can assess against precommitted rubrics after the listener answers are locked. AI simulation of a listener is not proof that human attendees understood. Report precise confusion points and suggested repairs, not scores alone.
